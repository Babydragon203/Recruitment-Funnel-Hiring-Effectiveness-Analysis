# Recruitment Funnel & Hiring Effectiveness Analysis

An Excel-based HR Analytics project focused on evaluating recruitment effectiveness using 7,167 recruitment records.

## Objective

To analyze hiring outcomes, identify variations in recruitment effectiveness across departments, examine compensation patterns, and evaluate gender-wise hiring outcomes to generate actionable HR recommendations.


## Key Analysis

- Overall hiring conversion and rejection rates
- Department-wise hiring effectiveness
- Department-wise recruitment volume
- Average offered salary by department
- Gender-wise hiring outcomes and conversion rates
- Recruitment KPI analysis

## Key Findings

- Overall hiring conversion rate: **65.52%**
- Total hired: **4,696**
- Total rejected: **2,471**
- HR Department recorded the highest hiring conversion rate: **72.16%**
- Finance Department recorded the lowest hiring conversion rate: **61.11%**
- Operations had the highest recruitment volume: **2,771 applicants**
- General Management had the highest average offered salary: **₹58,722**
- Female applicants recorded the highest hiring conversion rate: **69.38%**

## Tools Used

- Microsoft Excel
- Pivot Tables
- Data Cleaning
- KPI Analysis
- Data Visualization
- HR Analytics

## Deliverables

- Cleaned recruitment dataset
- Excel-based analysis
- Recruitment KPI dashboard
- Data visualizations
- HR recommendations
- Project presentation

## Business Recommendations

The analysis recommends replicating effective recruitment practices across departments, reviewing lower-conversion functions, strengthening recruitment capacity for high-volume departments, regularly benchmarking compensation, and implementing centralized HR analytics dashboards for ongoing recruitment monitoring.
